01 / WEB PROJECT · PUBLIC

My personal site

A coding project and a personal scrapbook in the same place. The site brings together project notes, music and film collections, games, stories, and a small setup journal—without losing its own handmade character.

It’s a multi-page static site, built directly with web fundamentals. The architecture keeps the workbench easy to grow while the archive gives the other interests their own space.

What it includes

  • A coding-first home page and project workbench
  • A separate personal archive for music, films, and imaginary worlds
  • A small responsive design system with light and dark themes
  • Handmade visual elements, clear navigation, and reduced-motion support
