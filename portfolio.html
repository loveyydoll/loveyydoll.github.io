A LITTLE CHARACTER IN THE KEYS

Aetherloom.

My own character synth. Something warm to get lost in, with room for rough edges and a little weirdness.

The first playable version has polyphonic voices, an envelope, a filter, and three starting colours: WARM, BLEND and GLITCH. PLAY keeps things simple; EDIT opens up the sound.
